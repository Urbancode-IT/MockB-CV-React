Career Change

✦ For switching industries 
Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my strong interest in the [Position] role at [Company]. While my background is in [Previous Industry], I have spent the past [X years] developing highly transferable skills in [Skill 1], [Skill 2], and [Skill 3] that I believe make me an excellent fit for this position.

During my time at [Previous Company], I [Achievement 1] and successfully [Achievement 2], demonstrating my ability to adapt and drive results in fast-paced environments. I am now eager to bring this expertise to the [Target Industry] sector.

I am excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Company]'s mission of [Company Mission/Value]. I would welcome the chance to discuss how my unique perspective can benefit your team.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Recent Graduate

✦ Entry level / First job 
Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the [Position] at [Company]. As a recent graduate with a degree in [Degree] from [University], I have built a strong foundation in [Field/Skill Area] through both my academic coursework and hands-on project experience.

My final-year project on [Project Name] gave me practical experience in [Relevant Skill], and my internship at [Internship Company] allowed me to apply this knowledge in a real-world setting, where I [Key Internship Achievement].

I am a fast learner, highly motivated, and passionate about [Industry/Role]. I would love the opportunity to grow with [Company] and contribute meaningfully to your team from day one.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

Senior Professional

✦ Leadership / Executive level 
Dear [Hiring Manager's Name],

I am writing to express my interest in the [Position] at [Company]. With over [X] years of progressive experience in [Industry], I have a proven record of delivering measurable results—including [Specific Achievement, e.g., "driving 30% revenue growth" or "leading a team of 50+ professionals"].

At [Current/Previous Company], I spearheaded [Major Initiative], which resulted in [Quantifiable Outcome]. My expertise in [Core Competency 1] and [Core Competency 2] has consistently enabled organizations to achieve their strategic objectives while building high-performing, collaborative teams.

I am drawn to [Company] because of [Specific Reason—e.g., its commitment to innovation, market leadership, etc.]. I am confident that my leadership experience and strategic mindset align perfectly with your organizational goals.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to [Company]'s continued success.

Respectfully,
[Your Name]

Remote / Freelance

✦ For remote positions 
Dear Hiring Manager,

I am thrilled to apply for the remote [Position] role at [Company]. As a self-motivated professional with [X] years of experience working in distributed and remote environments, I have perfected the skills needed to thrive and deliver outstanding results without direct supervision.

I have successfully [Key Remote Achievement], using tools such as [Tools, e.g., Slack, Asana, Figma, GitHub] to maintain seamless collaboration across time zones. My commitment to clear communication, accountability, and consistent delivery has made me a trusted partner on every remote project I have undertaken.

What excites me most about [Company] is [Specific Reason]. I am fully equipped with the home office setup, digital tools, and work ethic to make an immediate and lasting impact on your team.

Looking forward to connecting,
[Your Name]

Through my coursework in [Relevant Subject] and my involvement in [Club/Project/Activity], I have developed skills in [Skill 1] and [Skill 2]. I am eager to apply these skills in a professional setting and to learn from the talented team at [Company].

This internship would be a tremendous opportunity for me to grow as a professional while contributing fresh ideas and dedicated effort to your organization.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the possibility of joining your team.

Sincerely,

Got Questions?

We've got the answers to help you beat the ATS algorithms.

How does the AI calculate my ATS score?

Our AI uses natural language processing (NLP) to compare your letter against a database of 100,000+ job descriptions. It checks for keyword density, structural parsing (how a machine reads it), and even "power words" that trigger recruiter interest.

Is PDF better than Word for ATS?

While modern ATS can read both, a standard .docx file is often safer for complex layouts. However, our checker ensures your PDF is formatted with a "text layer" that makes it 100% readable by any system.

Can I re-check my letter after making changes?

Yes! You can upload your updated letter as many times as you want. We actually recommend iterating until you hit a score of 85 or higher for the best results.

Is my personal data kept private?

Absolutely. We use 256-bit SSL encryption. Your documents are only stored temporarily for analysis and are automatically purged from our servers to ensure your privacy.

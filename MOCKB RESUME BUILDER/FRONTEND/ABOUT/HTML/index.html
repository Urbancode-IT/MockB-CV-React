Our Story

MockB CV was created with one mission — helping professionals create resumes that actually get noticed.

Most job seekers struggle with ATS systems, poor formatting, weak content, and confusing tools. We wanted to simplify the process and bring every career-building tool into one intelligent platform.

Today, MockB CV helps students, freshers, professionals, freelancers, and job seekers create resumes, cover letters, and portfolios that stand out.

To empower job seekers worldwide with AI-driven tools that simplify resume creation and increase interview opportunities.

FAQs

How does the AI analyze the JD?

Our AI uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to identify core competencies, required technical stacks, and even the "hidden" requirements implied by the job responsibilities.

Is the cover letter really ATS-friendly?

Yes. We prioritize keyword density and formatting that standard ATS systems (like Workday, Greenhouse, or Lever) can easily parse and rank highly.

Can I edit the generated letter?

Absolutely! You can download it as a Word document (.doc) to make any personal manual tweaks before sending it off.

Does it work for all industries?

Our AI is trained on over 50,000 job descriptions across tech, finance, healthcare, marketing, and more, making it versatile for any career path.

What Recruiters Actually Look For

Recruiters scan resumes in 6–8 seconds. You must grab their attention instantly with job relevance, skills match, and clean formatting.

Scan-Ready

Perfect Resume Structure

Stick to a standard hierarchy: Header, Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, and Education. Avoid fancy graphics that confuse ATS.

ATS Friendly

How to Write Strong Content

Focus on results using the formula: Action Verb + Task + Result. Turn responsibilities into measurable achievements.

Result-Driven

Keywords (ATS Optimization)

Identify repeated terms in the Job Description and include them naturally. Match exact phrasing like "React.js" instead of just "React".
